The role

Abu Dhabi

Our purpose is to help clients approach opportunities with clearer information, realistic assumptions and the right local support.

K&K's value is not the promotion of a particular property or product. It is the ability to help an investor navigate the market, ask the right commercial questions and assemble the appropriate licensed team around the opportunity.

We do not replace licensed brokers, valuers, legal advisers, tax advisers, financial advisers or other regulated professionals. We help the investor coordinate them and keep the wider commercial objective in view.

Investor requirements & market orientation

Your objectives, time horizon, preferred sectors or property types, expected involvement, budget parameters and risk considerations, followed by an introduction to the relevant UAE market and operating context.

Opportunity screening

An initial commercial review of presented opportunities: location, positioning, sponsor or developer profile, demand assumptions, proposed commercial terms, operating considerations and evident risks.

Commercial assessment & scenario review

Financial assumptions, cash requirements, operating considerations and scenario comparisons, so the commercial logic behind an opportunity is understood. Formal valuation, investment advice and regulated financial analysis remain with appointed professionals.

Real estate & hospitality-led opportunities

Residential, commercial, mixed-use or hospitality-related opportunities, with particular strength where property, business operations and destination potential must be considered together. About K&K Hospitality

Partner & adviser introductions

Licensed brokers, developers, legal advisers, banks, tax advisers, valuers, technical consultants, property managers and other specialists, according to the mandate.

Due-diligence coordination

An information checklist, coordination of advisers and tracking of the key commercial, legal, financial and technical questions, so material issues are visible before a decision is taken.

Investor representation & project coordination

Ongoing coordination on your behalf through evaluation, negotiation support, adviser management and implementation, without undertaking activities reserved for licensed professionals.

Post-investment oversight

Periodic commercial reporting, stakeholder coordination and owner representation under an agreed scope, particularly where the investor is based outside the UAE or requires an independent local point of contact.
