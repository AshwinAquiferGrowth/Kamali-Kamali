Who we are

Kamali & Kamali Holding LLC works from both sides of the table: as an adviser and operator with hands-on experience, and as an investor and partner with capital and relationships to commit.

Four sectors, one way of working: understand the business before the deal, put the right licensed people around it, and stay involved after the signing. In hospitality we own and operate. In investment and real estate we sit on the investor's side. In AI and in education we help organisations move from an ambition to a plan that works.

Leadership

Dr. Tayeb Kamali

Founder
Education and investment

Dr. Kamali served as Vice Chancellor of the Higher Colleges of Technology, the UAE's largest higher-education institution, and established the CERT Technology Park — the region's first, with an engagement portfolio valued at over US$275 million. He chairs the board of the Abu Dhabi School of Management and serves on the boards of Gulf Islamic Investments, Vision Bank, and the Mohammed bin Rashid School of Government.

His contribution to education was recognised with France's highest academic honour, the Ordre des Palmes Académiques, and an honorary Doctorate in Engineering from the University of Waterloo.

Mohamed Kamali

Founder & Managing Director
Hospitality and ventures

Mohamed is an Emirati entrepreneur, hospitality operator and investor whose experience spans restaurant ownership, culinary operations, venture building and business advisory. His career began in the kitchen at Hayato in Los Angeles, where he rose through the brigade to sous chef; during his tenure the restaurant was awarded its second Michelin star. He later served as Shareholder and General Manager of Kohantei Dubai and now leads the development of Kohantei Abu Dhabi at The Galleria, Al Maryah Island.

Beyond hospitality, Mohamed was Managing Partner of Unity Consulting for a decade, co-founded VUL9 Security Solutions and remains active in venture capital and angel investment. He holds an MBA from Marymount California University and brings an owner’s perspective grounded in hands-on execution, commercial accountability and long-term value creation.
