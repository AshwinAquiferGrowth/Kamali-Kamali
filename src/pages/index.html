Leadership — the principals

Est. Abu Dhabi

Dr. Tayeb Kamali

Founder
Education · Investment

Dr. Kamali served as Vice Chancellor of the Higher Colleges of Technology, the UAE's largest higher-education institution, and established the CERT Technology Park — the region's first, with an engagement portfolio valued at over US$275 million. He chairs the board of the Abu Dhabi School of Management and serves on the boards of Gulf Islamic Investments, Vision Bank, and the Mohammed bin Rashid School of Government.

His contribution to education was recognised with France's highest academic honour, the Ordre des Palmes Académiques, and an honorary Doctorate in Engineering from the University of Waterloo.

Mohammed Kamali

Co-founder & Managing Director
Hospitality · Ventures

Mohammed is an entrepreneur in the premium food and beverage sector. He served as Shareholder and General Manager of Kohantei Dubai, and is currently developing a premium traditional Kaiseki and A5 Wagyu restaurant concept at The Galleria Al Maryah Island in Abu Dhabi — management rooted in hands-on kitchen experience as a Sous Chef at Hayato, Los Angeles.

An active venture capitalist and angel investor, he was Managing Partner of Unity Consulting for a decade, co-founded VUL9 Security Solutions, and holds an MBA from Marymount California University.
