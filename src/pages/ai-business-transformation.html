Opportunity & readiness assessment

A clear-eyed review of your objectives, workflows, data, technology and governance. You get a practical answer: where AI can help, where it cannot yet, and what to resolve first.

Use-case prioritisation

We look for the high-value applications across customer service, administration, reporting, knowledge, marketing, finance, operations and productivity, then rank them by value, feasibility, cost, risk and effort.

Strategy & roadmap

A phased plan with named priorities and responsibilities, the capabilities you will need, partner selection, governance, change management and how success will be measured.

Workflow transformation

We redesign selected workflows so that automation, data and AI tools take the repetitive work and people keep the judgement. Simpler work, more consistent results, time given back to teams.

Technology & partner coordination

We define requirements, evaluate providers and coordinate implementation with your appointed technical specialists, staying focused on business outcomes, operational fit and accountability.

Leadership, training & adoption

Executive briefings, management workshops and adoption programmes that help teams understand what AI can and cannot do in their roles, and how to use it responsibly.

Governance & performance review

Practical decision rights, review processes, usage principles and performance measures. Cybersecurity, data protection, legal and regulatory advice stay with the appointed specialists.

Leadership

AI adoption is led by strategy, governance and people who understand how the work changes. Not by technology alone.

Dr. Tayeb Kamali

Founder
Technology, education and institutions

Dr. Kamali has spent his career connecting education, applied research and technology with how organisations actually perform. As Vice Chancellor of the Higher Colleges of Technology and founding Group Chief Executive of the Centre of Excellence for Applied Research and Training, he led initiatives that turned emerging technology into practical learning and workforce capability at national scale.

That work included large-scale digital learning, applied technology partnerships and executive education. He chairs the Board of Trustees of the Abu Dhabi School of Management, supporting its programmes in business analytics, AI management, big data, entrepreneurship and innovation. It is the perspective behind K&K's approach.

Scope
