H.E. Dr. Tayeb Kamali

Co-Founder & Chairman
Education and institutional development

Dr. Kamali is one of the UAE's established leaders in higher education, applied research, training and institutional development. At the Higher Colleges of Technology and the Centre of Excellence for Applied Research and Training he built education, professional training, applied technology and industry-partnership initiatives at national and regional scale: workforce programmes with government entities, international academic relationships, technology-enabled learning, and education models designed around economic and employment needs.

He chairs the Board of Trustees of the Abu Dhabi School of Management, guiding its direction in entrepreneurship, leadership, innovation, sustainability, business analytics and AI-related management education. His contribution to education has been recognised with France's Ordre des Palmes Académiques and an honorary Doctorate in Engineering from the University of Waterloo. That experience, and the network that came with it, is what K&K brings to institutional work.
