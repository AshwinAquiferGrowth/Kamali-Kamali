K&K 
Education & Institutional Development 
Building institutions, capability and partnerships for long -term impact.  
Overview 
K&K supports education providers, government entities, investors and organisations developing 
learning, training and workforce-capability initiatives in the UAE and internationally. 
Our perspective combines institutional leadership, applied education, technology, executive 
development and public-private partnership experience. We help clients move from an ambition 
or partnership opportunity to a clear institutional model, implementation plan and network of 
relevant academic, government and industry stakeholders. 
What We Do 
Institutional Strategy & Development 
Support in defining institutional purpose, strategic priorities, operating model, governance 
considerations, growth direction and implementation roadmap for new or evolving education and 
training organisations. 
Programme & Portfolio Strategy 
Review and development of programme portfolios aligned with learner needs, workforce demand, 
industry priorities and institutional positioning. Academic approval, accreditation and quality 
assurance remain with the relevant institution and specialist authorities. 
Workforce Development & Applied Learning 
Design of training and capability-building initiatives that connect education with employment, 
organisational performance and national workforce priorities. 
Executive Education & Leadership Development 
Development and coordination of executive learning, leadership programmes, professional 
education and sector-specific training in partnership with qualified academic and delivery 
organisations. 
University, Government & Industry Partnerships 
Identification and development of collaborations between universities, government entities, 
employers, research centres, technology providers and international institutions. 
Innovation, Entrepreneurship & Incubation 
Support for innovation centres, entrepreneurship programmes, applied research partnerships, 
incubators and initiatives that help ideas move from education into commercial or societal 
application.

Digital Learning & AI in Education 
Strategic support for technology-enabled education, AI literacy, digital-learning transformation 
and the responsible integration of emerging tools into teaching, administration and institutional 
decision-making. 
International Market Entry & Collaboration 
Support for education and training organisations evaluating entry into the UAE or seeking 
regional partnerships, including opportunity assessment, stakeholder mapping, partnership 
development and local coordination. 
Institutional Review & Advisory 
Independent review of strategy, structure, partnerships, programme relevance and delivery 
priorities, translated into practical recommendations and an agreed action plan. 
How We Work 
K&K can undertake a defined strategic review, support the development of a programme or 
partnership, or provide continuing advisory and stakeholder coordination. Academic, 
accreditation, licensing and regulatory responsibilities remain with the institution and appropriately 
appointed specialists. 
Scope & Professional Boundaries 
K&K provides strategic, commercial and implementation support within an agreed mandate. 
Where specialist technical, legal, regulatory, financial or licensed professional services are 
required, these remain with appropriately qualified advisers and delivery partners.

Leadership & Relevant Experience 
H.E. Dr. Tayeb Kamali 
Co-Founder & Chairman 
Dr. Kamali is one of the UAE’s established leaders in higher education, applied research, training 
and institutional development. He previously served as Vice Chancellor of the Higher Colleges of 
Technology, the UAE’s federal applied higher-education system, and was the founding Group 
Chief Executive of the Centre of Excellence for Applied Research and Training, its commercial 
and training arm. 
Across these roles, he helped develop education, professional training, applied technology and 
industry-partnership initiatives at national and regional scale. His experience includes workforce-
development collaborations with government entities, international academic relationships, 
technology-enabled learning and the development of education models designed around 
economic and employment needs. 
He currently chairs the Board of Trustees of the Abu Dhabi School of Management, supporting its 
direction in entrepreneurship, leadership, innovation, sustainability, business analytics and AI-
related management education. This depth of experience provides K&K with a credible 
foundation for institutional strategy, partnership development and education-led transformation. 
Positioning Note 
This experience belongs to Dr. Kamali’s career across government, education, investment and 
institutional partnerships. It informs K&K’s capability and network; it should not be described as 
work previously delivered by K&K unless the relevant engagement was contracted through K&K.
