Overview 
K&K supports private investors, business owners and international partners exploring 
opportunities in the UAE. We provide local market perspective, commercial screening, structured 
coordination and access to an established network of licensed and specialist professionals. 
Our purpose is to help clients approach opportunities with clearer information, realistic 
assumptions and the right local support. We do not replace licensed brokers, valuers, legal 
advisers, tax advisers, financial advisers or other regulated professionals. We help the investor 
coordinate them and keep the wider commercial objective in view. 
What We Do 
Investor Requirements & Market Orientation 
Clarification of the investor’s objectives, time horizon, preferred sectors or property types, 
expected involvement, budget parameters and risk considerations, followed by an introduction to 
the relevant UAE market and operating context. 
Opportunity Screening 
Initial commercial review of presented opportunities, including location, positioning, sponsor or 
developer profile, demand assumptions, proposed commercial terms, operating considerations 
and evident risks. 
Commercial Assessment & Scenario Review 
Coordination of financial assumptions, cash requirements, operating considerations and scenario 
comparisons to help the client understand the commercial logic behind an opportunity. Formal 
valuation, investment advice and regulated financial analysis remain with appointed 
professionals. 
Real Estate & Hospitality-Led Opportunities 
Support for investors considering residential, commercial, mixed-use or hospitality-related 
opportunities, with particular strength where property, business operations and destination 
potential must be considered together. 
Partner & Adviser Introductions 
Introductions to licensed brokers, developers, legal advisers, banks, tax advisers, valuers, 
technical consultants, property managers and other relevant specialists according to the 
mandate.

Due-Diligence Coordination 
Development of an information checklist, coordination of advisers and tracking of key commercial, 
legal, financial and technical questions so that material issues are visible before a decision is 
taken. 
Investor Representation & Project Coordination 
Ongoing coordination on behalf of the client through evaluation, negotiation support, adviser 
management and implementation, without undertaking activities reserved for licensed 
professionals. 
Post-Investment Oversight 
Periodic commercial reporting, stakeholder coordination and owner representation under an 
agreed scope, particularly where the investor is based outside the UAE or requires an 
independent local point of contact. 
How We Work 
K&K can support a focused opportunity review, a broader UAE market-entry mandate or 
continuing owner representation. The scope is agreed around the client’s objectives and 
delivered in coordination with appropriately licensed brokers and professional advisers. 
Scope & Professional Boundaries 
K&K provides strategic, commercial and implementation support within an agreed mandate. 
Where specialist technical, legal, regulatory, financial or licensed professional services are 
required, these remain with appropriately qualified advisers and delivery partners.

Leadership & Relevant Experience

Co-Founder & Chairman
