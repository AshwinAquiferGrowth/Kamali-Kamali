Overview 
K&K helps organisations understand where artificial intelligence and digital tools can create 
genuine operational value. We focus on the business problem first: how work is performed, where 
time and information are lost, what decisions can be improved and whether the organisation is 
ready to adopt new technology responsibly. 
Our role is to connect leadership, operations and technology. We help define the opportunity, 
establish priorities, coordinate specialist partners and support implementation so that AI becomes 
a practical business capability rather than an isolated experiment. 
What We Do 
AI Opportunity & Readiness Assessment 
Review of business objectives, workflows, data availability, technology environment, governance 
requirements and organisational readiness. The outcome is a practical view of where AI may 
help, where it may not, and what must be resolved before implementation. 
Use-Case Identification & Prioritisation 
Identification and evaluation of high-value applications across customer service, administration, 
reporting, knowledge management, marketing, finance, operations and workforce productivity. 
Opportunities are prioritised according to potential value, feasibility, cost, risk and implementation 
effort. 
AI Strategy & Implementation Roadmap 
Development of a phased roadmap covering priorities, responsibilities, required capabilities, 
partner selection, governance, change management and measures of success. 
Workflow & Process Transformation 
Redesign of selected workflows to combine human judgement with automation, data and AI-
enabled tools. The objective is to simplify work, improve consistency and release teams from 
repetitive administrative activity. 
Technology & Partner Coordination 
Support in defining requirements, evaluating technology providers and coordinating 
implementation with appointed technical specialists. K&K remains focused on business 
outcomes, operational fit and accountability.

Leadership, Training & Adoption 
Executive briefings, management workshops and workforce adoption programmes that help 
teams understand the opportunity, limitations and responsible use of AI within their roles. 
Governance & Performance Review 
Development of practical decision rights, review processes, usage principles and performance 
measures. Specialist cybersecurity, data protection, legal and regulatory advice remains with the 
relevant appointed professionals. 
How We Work 
Engagements may begin with a focused assessment or workshop and progress into a defined 
transformation programme. K&K can act as strategic adviser, programme coordinator or owner’s 
representative alongside the organisation’s internal teams and appointed technology partners. 
Scope & Professional Boundaries 
K&K provides strategic, commercial and implementation support within an agreed mandate. 
Where specialist technical, legal, regulatory, financial or licensed professional services are 
required, these remain with appropriately qualified advisers and delivery partners.

Leadership & Relevant Experience 
H.E. Dr. Tayeb Kamali 
Co-Founder & Chairman 
Dr. Kamali’s career has consistently connected education, applied research, technology and 
institutional development. As former Vice Chancellor of the Higher Colleges of Technology and 
founding Group Chief Executive of the Centre of Excellence for Applied Research and Training, 
he led and supported initiatives designed to translate emerging technology into practical learning, 
workforce capability and organisational performance. 
His work has included large-scale digital-learning initiatives, applied technology partnerships and 
executive education. As Chairman of the Abu Dhabi School of Management’s Board of Trustees, 
he has continued to support programmes in business analytics, artificial intelligence 
management, big-data management, entrepreneurship and innovation. 
This perspective shapes K&K’s approach: AI adoption must be led by clear strategy, responsible 
governance and people who understand how the technology changes the work—not by 
technology alone. 
Positioning Note 
This experience belongs to Dr. Kamali’s career across government, education, investment and 
institutional partnerships. It informs K&K’s capability and network; it should not be described as 
work previously delivered by K&K unless the relevant engagement was contracted through K&K.
