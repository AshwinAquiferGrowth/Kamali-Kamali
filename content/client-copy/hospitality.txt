HOSPITALITY LANDING PAGE 
We know hospitality from both sides 
K&K Hospitality brings together ownership, investment, culinary experience, restaurant development and 
day-to-day operations. 
We are developing our own venues, starting with Kohantei at The Galleria Abu Dhabi. We also work with 
owners, investors, developers, landlords and hospitality businesses that need experienced support to 
assess an opportunity, develop a concept, open a venue or manage an existing operation. 
Our focus is not limited to one format. We are open to restaurants, cafes, food and beverage venues within 
hotels, mall-based concepts, mixed-use destinations and the right opportunities in the UAE, across the GCC 
and internationally. 
The format may change. The principles do not. The concept must make sense for its market, the 
commercial structure must be workable and the operation must be built properly. 
What we want to build 
Our ambition is to build a focused portfolio of hospitality businesses through direct ownership, investment 
partnerships, joint ventures, management agreements and selected operating opportunities. 
We are not interested in collecting concepts for the sake of appearing bigger. We would rather be closely 
involved in a smaller number of venues where we understand the product, the numbers, the people and 
the guest experience. 
Where we can be involved 
• Restaurants and destination dining 
• Cafes and more accessible everyday concepts 
• Restaurants, cafes, bars and other food and beverage venues located within hotels 
• Hospitality concepts within malls and mixed-use developments 
• Partnerships with owners, investors, landlords and developers 
• International brands entering the UAE or GCC 
• Regional concepts considering international expansion 
• Existing venues that need repositioning, stronger controls or experienced management

OUR FIRST VENUE 
Kohantei 
Kohantei is the first restaurant being developed under K&K Hospitality. It is currently in development and 
pre-opening at The Galleria Abu Dhabi. 
For us, Kohantei is more than a name in a portfolio. It is the first full example of how we want to work - 
from the original opportunity and commercial discussions through development, opening and operation. 
Securing and progressing the location 
K&K has been closely involved in securing the opportunity and moving the project forward with the 
landlord and the wider project team. 
Our work has included: 
• Reviewing the opportunity and its commercial potential 
• Negotiating and coordinating rent and lease-related matters 
• Negotiating the landlord capital contribution 
• Clarifying landlord and tenant responsibilities 
• Maintaining direct liaison with the landlord 
• Coordinating decisions, dependencies and project priorities 
• Representing the operational needs of the restaurant during development 
• Preparing the team, systems and operating structure for opening 
Commercial details published on the website will remain limited to information K&K is permitted to disclose. 
From development to operation 
Kohantei is not being developed simply to reach an opening date. The work is being done with the intention 
of operating the restaurant properly after opening. 
Until Kohantei officially opens, we will describe it as being in development and pre-opening. Once it is trading, the website can be 
updated to present it as the first operating restaurant within K&K Hospitality.

WHAT WE PROVIDE 
Hospitality consultancy, development 
and management 
The same owner and operator thinking used in our own projects is available to selected clients. 
We can become involved at the beginning of an idea, during development, before opening or after a venue 
is already operating. The role depends on what the owner needs and how much responsibility K&K is 
expected to take. 
Strategy, positioning and feasibility 
Before committing significant time and capital, we help the owner understand whether the opportunity 
makes sense and how it should be approached. 
This can include the market, location, audience, concept positioning, commercial assumptions, operating 
model and the practical requirements behind the idea. 
Concept and restaurant development 
We help turn the idea into a restaurant or hospitality concept that can actually be built and operated. 
This may cover the operating brief, guest journey, service model, restaurant flow, kitchen and operational 
requirements, equipment priorities, opening budgets and coordination with appointed designers and 
specialists. 
Pre-opening and launch 
Opening preparation is where many projects become rushed and disconnected. We bring the workstreams 
together and keep the operation at the centre of the process. 
Our involvement may include the pre-opening plan, manpower, recruitment, operating procedures, 
suppliers, systems, training, budgets, opening inventories, readiness reviews and soft-opening support.

WHAT WE PROVIDE 
Support after the doors open 
Operations and commercial performance 
For an operating venue, we look beyond surface-level sales. We review how the business is being managed 
and where commercial or operational attention is required. 
This may include revenue, food and beverage cost, labour, purchasing, inventory, menu performance, 
service standards, guest feedback, reporting and management accountability. 
Owner representation 
Not every owner wants or is able to remain inside every project discussion. We can represent the owner's 
interests across agreed commercial, development and operating matters. 
The role may include landlord liaison, coordination with appointed consultants, monitoring key decisions 
and dependencies, reviewing progress and providing the owner with a clear and independent view of the 
project or operation. 
Restaurant and venue management 
Where K&K accepts a management appointment, we take a direct role in running the business under an 
agreed structure of authority and accountability. 
This may include leadership, budgeting, financial controls, people management, purchasing, service 
standards, commercial reporting and the day-to-day operation of the venue. 
A management appointment is different from consultancy. It involves continuing responsibility for the 
business, not only advice or a set of project deliverables. 
Market entry and expansion 
We are open to working with hospitality brands entering the UAE or GCC, as well as regional concepts 
considering expansion into other markets. 
Our role may include assessing the opportunity, local positioning, operating requirements, development 
planning, pre-opening and implementation. Legal, tax, franchise documentation and authority approvals 
remain with the appropriate appointed professionals.

LEADERSHIP 
Two perspectives, one business 
K&K Hospitality is led by Mohamed Kamali and Mihai Giurebe. Their backgrounds are different, and that is 
exactly the value of the partnership. 
Mohamed Kamali 
Managing Director | Investment and Hospitality Development 
Mohamed brings the perspective of an owner and investor, but his understanding of hospitality is not only 
financial. 
He previously worked as a chef in Los Angeles under a renowned Michelin-starred chef. That experience 
gave him a hands-on understanding of kitchen discipline, product quality, culinary standards and the work 
required behind the scenes to deliver a strong guest experience. 
At K&K, Mohamed is involved in assessing opportunities, investment strategy, commercial structure, 
concept positioning, culinary relationships and long-term development. He is closely involved in Kohantei 
from both the ownership and concept side of the business. 
Mihai Giurebe 
General Manager | Hospitality Operations and Development 
Mihai brings extensive restaurant development and operating experience from the UAE and GCC. 
His experience includes leading teams of more than 100 employees, developing and opening premium 
restaurant concepts in Dubai, and leading a major restaurant opening that received Michelin Opening of 
the Year recognition. 
He currently leads the development and pre-opening of Kohantei at The Galleria Abu Dhabi, including 
project coordination, landlord liaison, operating requirements, team development, systems and opening 
readiness. 
How the partnership works 
Mohamed looks at the opportunity as an investor, owner and someone who has worked inside a 
professional kitchen. Mihai looks at how the concept will be developed, opened, staffed, controlled and 
operated every day. 
Together, they can follow the full journey - from the first discussion and investment decision to opening, 
operation and future growth.
