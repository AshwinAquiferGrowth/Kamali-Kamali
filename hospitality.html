What we provide
Consultancy · Development · Management

The same owner-and-operator thinking used in our own projects is available to selected clients — at the beginning of an idea, during development, before opening or after a venue is already operating. The role depends on what the owner needs and how much responsibility K&K is expected to take.

01
Strategy, positioning & feasibility

Before committing significant time and capital, we help the owner understand whether the opportunity makes sense and how it should be approached — the market, location, audience, concept positioning, commercial assumptions, operating model and the practical requirements behind the idea.

02
Concept & restaurant development

We help turn the idea into a restaurant or hospitality concept that can actually be built and operated — the operating brief, guest journey, service model, restaurant flow, kitchen and operational requirements, equipment priorities, opening budgets and coordination with appointed designers and specialists.

03
Pre-opening & launch

Opening preparation is where many projects become rushed and disconnected. We bring the workstreams together and keep the operation at the centre — the pre-opening plan, manpower, recruitment, operating procedures, suppliers, systems, training, budgets, opening inventories, readiness reviews and soft-opening support.

04
Operations & commercial performance

For an operating venue, we look beyond surface-level sales at how the business is being managed and where attention is required — revenue, food and beverage cost, labour, purchasing, inventory, menu performance, service standards, guest feedback, reporting and management accountability.

05
Owner representation

Not every owner wants — or is able — to remain inside every project discussion. We represent the owner's interests across agreed commercial, development and operating matters: landlord liaison, coordination with appointed consultants, monitoring key decisions, reviewing progress and providing a clear, independent view.

06
Restaurant & venue management

Where K&K accepts a management appointment, we take a direct role in running the business under an agreed structure of authority and accountability — leadership, budgeting, financial controls, people, purchasing, service standards and commercial reporting. A management appointment involves continuing responsibility for the business, not only advice.

07
Market entry & expansion

For hospitality brands entering the UAE or GCC, and regional concepts expanding into new markets — assessing the opportunity, local positioning, operating requirements, development planning, pre-opening and implementation. Legal, tax, franchise documentation and authority approvals remain with the appropriate appointed professionals.
