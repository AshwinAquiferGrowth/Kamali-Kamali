How we work with you
Five ways

The same owner-and-operator thinking behind our own venues is available to selected clients — from the first idea to an operating business. The role depends on what the owner needs and how much responsibility K&K is expected to take.

01
Investment & partnership
Ownership · Joint ventures · Operating agreements

For selected opportunities where K&K invests, partners, develops or operates — a focused portfolio of venues we are closely involved in, rather than a long list of concepts.

02
Consultancy
Strategy · Feasibility · Concept

A defined review before significant time and capital is committed: the market, location, audience, concept positioning, commercial assumptions and operating model behind the idea.

03
Development & pre-opening
Operating brief · Pre-opening · Launch

Practical leadership from operating brief to opening day — guest journey, kitchen and operational requirements, budgets, recruitment, suppliers, systems, training and readiness — keeping the operation at the centre of a process that too often becomes rushed.

04
Owner representation
Oversight · Coordination · Reporting

An independent view for owners who cannot be inside every discussion: landlord liaison, coordination of appointed consultants, monitoring of key decisions and clear reporting on the project or the operating business.

05
Management
Operations · Performance · Accountability

Direct responsibility for running the venue under an agreed structure of authority — leadership, financial controls, people, purchasing, service standards and commercial reporting. Continuing responsibility for the business, not only advice.

Market entry

For brands entering the UAE or GCC and regional concepts expanding abroad, we combine these roles as the opportunity requires. Legal, tax, franchise documentation and authority approvals remain with the appropriate appointed professionals.
